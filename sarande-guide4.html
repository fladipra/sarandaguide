The Best Beaches

💡 Local tip: Go early (before 10am) or late afternoon — midday sun is intense. Always bring water with you!
Krorëz Beach
Hidden gem
One of our favorite hidden spots near Sarandë. It takes a bit of effort to get there, but that’s what keeps it quiet and untouched. The water is incredibly clear, and the whole place feels peaceful and wild at the same time. Diving is amazing — bring a mask
⚓ The water way🤿 Snorkelling spot📍 Tucked away
Shpella e pellumbave
Scenic
Another of our top picks we want something special. The water is incredibly clear, and the whole place feels calm and untouched compared to the busy beaches. It’s perfect if you just want to swim, relax, and enjoy nature. Get your legs ready for the steps! Same as the previous, don't forget a mask.
🚗 10 min by taxi🤿 Snorkelling spot📍 In the middle of nowhere
Orange Beach Club
🔥 Alive
You’ve got to hit Orange Beach Club if you want an atmosphere that actually lives up to the hype. There is nothing like being in the middle of that open-air energy just as the music peaks and the sun hits the waves. It’s polished but still wild enough to keep you there long after dark. If you want the one spot in Ksamil that feels truly electric, that’s the move.
🚌 Bus from centre (€2)⭐ Don't miss📍 Shorefront energy
Kodër Beach
🚶 Walking
f you're looking for a spot that feels a bit more "local" and lived-in, you should definitely check out Kodër Beach. It’s right on the edge of the city, so you get this great mix of convenience without the intense tourist-trap feel of the main strip. The water is surprisingly clear for being so close to town, and there are some fantastic little seafood spots tucked right along the shore. Honestly, if you want a day that feels easy, authentic, and just a few steps away from where everything is happening, Kodër is a solid choice.
📍 Just around the corner🌿 Very peaceful

Things To Do

💡 Local tip: The ancient ruins of Butrint are only 20 min south and completely unmissable. Budget a half-day for it.
Butrint National Park
UNESCO
Trust me, don’t miss Butrint National Park. One minute you’re surrounded by quiet, untouched nature, and the next you’re walking through 2,500-year-old Greek and Roman ruins—temples, theatres, even a Byzantine basilica. It’s peaceful, scenic, and feels completely different from the typical beach spots around here, like you’ve stepped into another time..
🚌 Bus, ~€2🎟 Entrance ~€(free Sundays)⏱ Half-day📍 Location
Lëkurësi Castle
Views
A 16th-century Ottoman castle above Sarandë with 360° views over the bay and Corfu Island. Breathtaking at sunset.
🚗 10 min by taxi🌅 Best at sunset🆓 Free entry
Blue Eye Spring
Nature
A mystical natural spring in the mountains — the water is so blue it looks unreal. A cold, refreshing swim surrounded by forested hills.
🚗 ~1hr by car🌿 Nature reserve🎟 Small entry fee
