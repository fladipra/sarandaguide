Eat & Drink

💡 Local tip: Cash is king.👑 ALWAYS have some with you, ditch your credit card.
Finest Grill
Grilled
Skip the tourist strip and find yourself a real grill restaurant, the kind where the meat comes straight off the charcoal and the portions make you question your life choices. Finest Grill is the real deal. No gimmicks, just perfectly seasoned meat, cold beer, and flawless local hospitality. If you leave here hungry, that's entirely on you.

P.S. Try the finest salad and fries. You WON'T regret it!
📍 Gjergj Araniti St💰 Budget-friendly
Restaurant Te Bequa
Albanian
If you’re trying to experience real Albanian food, not the polished tourist version, go to Te Bequa. It’s one of those places that doesn’t try too hard, because it doesn’t have to, the recipes speak for themselves. Out of all the “traditional” spots, this one just feels the most genuine. And whatever you do, don’t leave without trying Tavë kosi, it’s rich, comforting, and probably the most authentic dish you’ll find in Albania.
📍 Center🧑‍🍳Traditional
Taverna Labëria
Grilled & skewered
Taverna Labëria is one of those places I keep going back to when I want something that actually feels real. They’ve got a bit of everything on the menu, but the moment you try their grilled and skewered meats, you understand what they’re really about. It’s not just Albanian food in general, it’s proper Labëria-style cooking, with that rich, smoky flavor and simple recipes done right. Everything tastes like it’s been made with care, not rushed for tourists. The whole place has this warm, local feel where you can just sit, eat properly, and enjoy it without overthinking anything.
📍 Mitat Hoxha St🍖 Laberia tradition
Green Life Market
Plant based
Well of course we could never forget about all of our vegan guests and clients and I have just the place for you. Green Life Market isn’t just a quick stop, it’s a proper spot where y'all can actually sit down and enjoy a full meal without feeling limited. The menu is built around fresh, plant-based options that don’t feel like compromises, you’re getting real dishes, real flavors, and a proper dining experience. It’s the kind of place where can eat normally, comfortably, and actually have choices, not just sides. Simple, fresh, and genuinely satisfying.
📍 Just there🫑 Vegan

The food is incredibly cheap and satisfying, making it a go-to for an easy meal. Best of all, you get to enjoy your food alongside a beautiful, sweeping view of the town and the sea.

Taverna Fish Filipi

If you’re craving seafood in Sarandë, this place is one I’d actually recommend. It’s the kind of spot where you just sit back, enjoy good food, and don’t overthink it. Definitely worth it if you want a proper seaside meal.

The Best Beaches

💡 Local tip: Go early (before 10am) or late afternoon — midday sun is intense. Always bring water with you!
Krorëz Beach
Hidden gem
One of our favorite hidden spots near Sarandë. It takes a bit of effort to get there, but that’s what keeps it quiet and untouched. The water is incredibly clear, and the whole place feels peaceful and wild at the same time. Diving is amazing, bring a mask
⚓ The water way🤿 Snorkelling spot📍 Tucked away
Shpella e pellumbave
Scenic
Another of our top picks we want something special. The water is incredibly clear, and the whole place feels calm and untouched compared to the busy beaches. It’s perfect if you just want to swim, relax, and enjoy nature. Get your legs ready for the steps! Same as the previous, don't forget a mask.
🚗 10 min by taxi🤿 Snorkelling spot📍 In the middle of nowhere
Orange Beach Club
🔥 Alive
You’ve got to hit Orange Beach Club if you want an atmosphere that actually lives up to the hype. There is nothing like being in the middle of that open-air energy just as the music peaks and the sun hits the waves. It’s polished but still wild enough to keep you there long after dark. If you want the one spot in Ksamil that feels truly electric, that’s the move.
🚌 Bus from centre (€2)⭐ Don't miss📍 Shorefront energy
Mirror beach
Just a vibe
This place actually hits the first time you see it. The water is ridiculously clear, like you can see everything, and when the sun hits it right, it really does look like a mirror. It’s a bit tucked away, so it doesn’t feel as chaotic as the main spots, but it still has a nice energy. Go a little earlier or later and it’s honestly one of those places you just end up staying way longer than you planned.
🌊 Turqoise water📍 Bus ride
Pulebardha beach
🐚 Lowkey
This is one of those spots you don’t just stumble on, you kind of have to know it’s there. Tucked away and way quieter than the usual beaches, it feels a bit more untouched and personal. The water is insanely clear, and the whole place has that calm, almost private vibe. The bus line to Ksamil passes by this place, so I guess thats the best way to get there. Definitely one of those places you go when you want to escape the crowds for a bit. Its basically Mirror Beach, but scaled down on the crowds.
📍 Unknown location🌿 Very peaceful🤿 Snorkelling spot
Mucobega beach
Just a vibe
If you’re looking for a more relaxed beach away from the busy spots, you should check out Mucobega Beach. It’s got that calm, laid-back vibe where you can actually unwind, enjoy the clear water, and not feel overwhelmed by crowds. It’s not trying to be anything fancy, and that’s exactly the charm, just clean water, a quieter atmosphere, and space to properly relax. Perfect if you want a slower, more peaceful beach day.

Things To Do

💡 Local tip: The ancient ruins of Butrint are only 20 min south and completely unmissable. Budget a half-day for it.
Butrint National Park
UNESCO
Trust me, don’t miss Butrint National Park. One minute you’re surrounded by quiet, untouched nature, and the next you’re walking through 2,500-year-old Greek and Roman ruins: temples, theatres, even a Byzantine basilica. It’s peaceful, scenic, and feels completely different from the typical beach spots around here, like you’ve stepped into another time.

P.S. If you're lucky enough to visit Saranda at the end of the month, the entry for Butrint is free for every LAST Sunday of the month.
🚌 Bus, ~€2🎟 Entrance ~€10⏱ Half-day📍 Location
Lëkurësi Castle
Views
You should definitely head up to Lëkurësi Castle at least once. It’s a 16th-century Ottoman castle sitting above the city, and the views are unreal, you get a full 360° look over Sarandë and even across to Corfu. Go around sunset if you can, the whole place lights up and the atmosphere is just incredible.
🚗 10 min by taxi🌅 Best at sunset🆓 Free entry📍 Location
Gjirokastër
Day trip escape
Gjirokastër is a perfect day trip if you want a break from the coast and something more authentic, with its stone houses, narrow streets, and the castle overlooking the city giving it a real "step back in time" feel. It’s the kind of place you explore slowly, just walking and taking everything in. The food is also part of the experience, with traditional dishes and rich homemade flavors, but it’s worth being selective since some touristy spots don’t always match the quality, find a more authentic place and it can easily become one of the highlights of your visit.
📍 Back in history🏛️ Old town vibes
Blue Eye Spring
Nature
You should definitely check out Blue Eye (Syri i Kaltër) while you’re here. It’s this hidden spring up in the mountains, and the water is so insanely blue it almost doesn’t look real. You’re not allowed to swim right at the source, but if you walk a bit further down the stream, you can take a dip there. It’s freezing cold, but super refreshing. Plus, the whole area is surrounded by quiet forest, so it really feels like a peaceful escape.
🚗 ~40min by car🌿 Nature reserve🎟 Small entry fee📍 Location

More Information

🚌Intercity buses
If you’re trying to get around Albania or even cross borders, intercity buses are your best option. In Sarandë, these buses leave from the terminal just outside the town. There are usually a couple of main agencies running routes Albania, connecting places like Tirana, Vlorë, and Gjirokastër, with buses running daily and at very affordable prices. For Greece, there are also a few agencies offering direct routes (like to Athens), usually once or twice a day, depending on the season. If you’re planning to go further, like Kosovo, North Macedonia, or Montenegro, there aren’t always physical agencies in town, but you can easily find routes and schedules online. Just keep in mind that in Albania, times aren’t always exact, and some buses leave when they’re full rather than on strict schedules, so it’s always good to double-check locally.
📍 Bus Terminal🌐 Online tickets
Bus Sarande-Ksamil-Butrint
Sarandë and Ksamil feel like two different places, but at the same time they're basically connected, which is why public transport between them is so easy. Buses run regularly throughout the day, especially in summer, and it's one of the simplest and cheapest ways to move between the city and the beaches. You don't really need to plan much, just head to the main road, hop on, and you'll be there in no time.

Ferries
One of the easiest things to do from Sarandë is head over to Corfu. Boats run daily, especially in summer, from early morning until the evening, so you’ll always find something that works. You can go for a speedboat if you want to get there quicker, or take the regular ferry which is slower but cheaper, it just depends on what you’re feeling. There are a few different companies running the route, and tickets are easy to sort once you’re in town. Prices are usually higher online, so it’s better to just get them directly at one of the offices. It’s honestly one of those super simple trips, you’re in another country before it even feels like you left.

ATMs and withdrawing money
Getting cash in Sarandë is easy, but choosing the right ATM can save you some money. From what we've seen amongs other tourists, go for Intesa Sanpaolo or BKT when using a foreign card. We recommend withdrawing in ALL (Lek) and always declining the ATM’s currency conversion (DCC). Let your own bank handle the exchange rate instead. Always check the fee before confirming, as your bank may also charge an additional fee. Also, check the fee before accepting the withdrawal. And don’t rely completely on your card — having some Lek in your wallet is always handy in Sarandë, especially for taxis, small shops and beach spots.
